    Round 2Packaging line QCFixed windows, content moves behind

    Go/no-go overlay gauge

    Each carton stops behind a steel template. Every window is cut to the tolerance for one feature. If the whole feature shows inside its window, it passes. If the plate hides any of it, it's out of spec.

    Carton 1 of 5
    Inspector

    Carton in position. Every feature must sit fully inside its window.

    Storyline: reproduces 1:1Draw the plate, draw four tolerance rectangles, Union the rectangles, then Subtract them from the plate. The carton is one object on a motion path behind it, with one state per defect.
    Why masking does the work: The window arrangement is the spec. Learners don't judge "does that look crooked." They judge "is the whole mark in the window," which is how physical overlay gauges work on real lines. Lift the template after a carton and the skewed label looks almost fine to the eye. That gap is the lesson.

    Storyline: partly reproducibleUse Merge Shapes → Fragment to split the drywall along your cut lines. Each fragment becomes its own shape you can hide on click, so learner-chosen cuts work fine. What doesn't port: the hole growing open as one continuous shape. Storyline only gives you static shapes, so fake the grow with a Zoom entrance on a cut-edge graphic.
